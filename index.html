I am an interdisciplinary researcher with over eight years of experience at the intersection of rehabilitation engineering, artificial intelligence, machine learning, physiological signal modelling, and digital health. My work focuses on developing AI-driven methods using EEG, sEMG, and motion-capture data to create digital biomarkers, quantitative assessment tools, and closed-loop rehabilitation platforms for precision therapy. I aim to translate clinically relevant technologies into meaningful solutions for people with neurological disorders and ageing populations.
